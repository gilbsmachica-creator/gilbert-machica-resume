### 3. Activate GitHub Pages
1. Look at the top horizontal menu bar of your repository screen and click the ⚙️ **Settings** tab.
2. In the left-hand sidebar layout, scroll down to the *Code and automation* section and click on **Pages**.
3. Under the **Build and deployment** section, look for **Source** (it should default to "Deploy from a branch").
4. Under the **Branch** dropdown menu, change it from *None* to **`main`** (or `master`), leave the folder as `/ (root)`, and click **Save**.
